### High-priority themes
1. **Missing transactions around multi-step mutations** — `demote_admin`, `disable_user`, `rotate_invite_code`, `heartbeat`, `sweep_expired`, `start_attempt`, `submit_quiz/annotation` all perform read-then-write without a `BEGIN IMMEDIATE`. These are the dominant bug class.
2. **Incomplete lock re-check inside BEGIN IMMEDIATE** — `save_annotation` and `set_complete` do a pre-txn ownership check but do not re-verify inside the write transaction, making the guard advisory only.
3. **Mirror dead-letter has no operator escape hatch** — rows that exhaust retries are permanently stranded with no documented or implemented re-queue path.
